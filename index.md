## Research Overview
As a systems researcher, I build interactive systems that **enhance visual accessibility** for blind and low vision (BLV) users and **augment human capabilities** for broader populations. My work centers on the following directions:

**1. Multi-Modal AI Systems for Accessible Visual Media**: 
I develop multi-modal machine learning pipelines to help BLV users engage with different types of visual media, such as online video comments (<a href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3706598.3713496" target="_blank">CHI 2025, best paper honorable mention</a>), 360° videos (UIST 2025, to appear), and personal photo collections (<a href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3654777.3676336" target="_blank">UIST 2024</a>).

**2. Multi-Sensory Interfaces for Real World Navigation**:
I develop multi-sensory wearable interfaces to support real-world navigation for BLV users, including on-body haptics (<a href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3411814" target="_blank">IMWUT 2020</a>), handheld vibrations (<a href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3411764.3445644" target="_blank">CHI 2021</a>), and head-mounted light guidance (<a href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3463524" target="_blank">IMWUT 2021</a>).

**3. Human-AI Systems for Augmenting Human Abilities**:
Beyond accessibility, I conduct collaborative research on human-AI systems that enhance human abilities in skill training and problem-solving (two collaborative papers at UIST 2025, to appear).
