## About Me
I'm a PhD student in <a href="https://cse.hkust.edu.hk/" target="_blank">Computer Science</a> at <a href="https://hkust.edu.hk/" target="_blank">the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology</a>, advised by Prof. <a href="http://www.huamin.org/" target="_blank">Huamin Qu</a> at <a href="http://vis.cse.ust.hk/" target="_blank">VisLab</a>. My research focuses on **AI-Powered interactive systems for enhancing visual accessibility**, with an emphasis on leveraging **multi-modal machine learning** and **multi-sensory interfaces** to make digital media and physical environments more inclusive and engaging for blind and low-vision (BLV) users.

My research enables BLV users to engage with a wide range of media, including photo collections (<a href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3654777.3676336" target="_blank">UIST 2024</a>), online videos (<a href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3706598.3713496" target="_blank">CHI 2025</a>), and 360° videos (UIST 2025, to appear). I also develop wearable technologies to support safe overdoor navigation for BLV users through multi-sensory feedback, including on-body haptics (<a href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3411814" target="_blank">IMWUT 2020</a>), handheld vibrations (<a href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3411764.3445644" target="_blank">CHI 2021</a>), and head-mounted light guidance (<a href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3463524" target="_blank">IMWUT 2021</a>).

Before starting my PhD, I earned both my bachelor's and master's degrees from <a href="https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/" target = "_blank">Tsinghua University</a>, including an M.S. in <a href="https://www.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/csen/" target="_blank">Computer Science</a> (advised by Prof. <a href= "https://pi.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn" target = "_blank">Yuanchun Shi</a>), a B.E. in <a href="https://www.eea.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/" target="_blank">Electrical Engineering</a>, and a second B.A. in <a href="https://www.enad.tsinghua.edu.cn/" target="_blank">Digital Meida Art</a>. I also spent two years working in the industry as a software product manager at <a href="https://www.mi.com/global/about/" target="_blank">Xiaomi Corporation</a>.

## Research Interests
**1. AI-Powered Interactive Systems for Visual Accessibility**: I focus on leveraging multi-modal machine learning and multi-sensory feedback to enhance visual accessibility in both digital and physical spaces.

**2. Human-AI Collaboration for Augmenting Human Abilities**: I also work on human-AI collaborative systems that augment human abilities in critical domains such as skill-learning and problem-solving (two collaborated UIST 2025 papers, to appear).
