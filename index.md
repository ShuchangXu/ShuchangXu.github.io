## Research Overview
My research centers on developing AI-powered interactive systems that augment **human perception and cognition** for blind and aging populations. I pursue this vision through three core areas:

**1. Personalized Memory Agents for Reminiscence**:
I develop personal memory agents that help users relive meaningful memories across diverse settings: (1) independent reminiscence with proactive chatbots (<a href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3654777.3676336" target="_blank">UIST 2024</a>), (2) collaborative reminiscence with therapist-in-the-loop support in dementia care, and (3) everyday reminiscence support integrated into daily routines. These systems incorporate cognitive science knowledge into AI agents to make reminiscence emotionally resonant and engaging.

**2. Multi-Modal AI Systems for Video Accessibility**:
I build machine learning pipelines that enable blind and low vision users to understand and engage with video content. Projects include making online video comments accessible (<a href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3706598.3713496" target="_blank">CHI 2025, best paper honorable mention</a>), enabling interactive exploration for 360° videos (<a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09959" target="_blank">UIST 2025</a>), and enhancing spatial awareness in film and television shows (<a href="https://shuchangxu.github.io/Sonic_Stage_Demo/" target="_blank">Sonic Stage</a>). These systems leverage multi-modal machine learning and expressive audio feedback to create immersive viewing experiences for blind viewers.

**3. Multi-Sensory Interfaces for Outdoor Navigation**:
I develop wearable systems that support navigation for blind and low vision users through multi-sensory feedback, such as on-body haptics (<a href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3411814" target="_blank">IMWUT 2020</a>), handheld vibrations (<a href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3411764.3445644" target="_blank">CHI 2021</a>), and head-mounted light cues (<a href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3463524" target="_blank">IMWUT 2021</a>). These systems provide real-time directional feedback to help users navigate independently and accurately.
