## Research Overview
As a systems researcher in Human-Computer Interaction, I design and build interactive systems that enhance visual accessibility for blind and low vision (BLV) users and augment human capabilities more broadly. My work spans three key areas:

**1. Multi-Modal AI Systems for Accessible Visual Media**: 
I develop **multi-modal machine learning** pipelines to help BLV users engage with diverse types of visual media, such as photo collections (<a href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3654777.3676336" target="_blank">UIST 2024</a>), online video comments (<a href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3706598.3713496" target="_blank">CHI 2025</a>, best paper honorable mention), 360° videos (UIST 2025, to appear). 

**2. Multi-Sensory Interfaces for Navigating the Real World**:
I develop **multi-sensory wearable interfaces** to support safe real-world navigation for BLV users, including on-body haptics (<a href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3411814" target="_blank">IMWUT 2020</a>), handheld vibrations (<a href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3411764.3445644" target="_blank">CHI 2021</a>), and head-mounted light guidance (<a href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3463524" target="_blank">IMWUT 2021</a>).

**3. Human-AI Systems for Augmenting Human Abilities**:
Beyond accessibility, I collaborate on human-AI systems research that enhance human abilities in domains such as skill training and problem-solving (two collaborative UIST 2025 papers, to appear).
