Research Area

My research builds AI-powered assistive systems that augment human perception and cognition, with a focus on enhancing accessibility for blind and aging populations. I combine methods from human-computer interaction, multi-modal machine learning, and accessible computing, spanning personalized memory agents for reminiscence, video accessibility, and multi-sensory interfaces for navigation.
